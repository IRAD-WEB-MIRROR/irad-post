Pensions and allowances

Irad Pochtasy pays state pensions and allowances in som at its counters on behalf of the Social Fund. Payment begins on the 10th of each month; if the 10th falls on a Sunday or holiday, on the next working day.

What to bring

  • Your ID card
  • Your pension book or allowance certificate
  • For someone else: a power of attorney certified by a notary or the district office

Delivery to the home

Pensioners over 80 and people with a disability of group I can have their pension brought home by the postman, free of charge. Apply once at your post office with a medical certificate.

To a bank account

Pensions can instead be paid into an account at Talasbank, Ak Bata Bank or Chuy Commercial Bank. Ask the Social Fund at your district office.

This month

Queues are longest on the first two days. From the third day most offices have no wait.
