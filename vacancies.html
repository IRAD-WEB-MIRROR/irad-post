How to apply

Send a short application with your CV, a copy of your ID card and copies of your diplomas to the personnel department: Directorate of Irad Pochtasy, Pochtamt Street 2, Talas, or hr@post.iradgov.org. Quote the reference number.

Applicants who meet the requirements are invited to an interview within two weeks of the closing date. Questions: +996-39-2-21-00-83.
