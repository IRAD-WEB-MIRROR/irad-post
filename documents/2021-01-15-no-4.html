Order of the Director General · 15 January 2021 · No. 4

Procedure for paying pensions and allowances at post offices

In force

1. State pensions and allowances are paid at post office counters in som from the 10th of each month. When the 10th is a Sunday or a holiday, payment begins on the next working day.

2. On payment days the Talas Central Post Office and the main offices of Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl open at 8:00.

3. The recipient presents an ID card and the pension book or allowance certificate.

4. Payment to another person is made on a power of attorney certified by a notary or the district office, or on form P-27 certified at the post office.

5. Pensioners over 80 and people with a disability of group I receive their pension at home from the postman, free of charge, on a single application with a medical certificate.

6. Sums not collected in the month are kept until the end of the following month.

Director General of Irad Pochtasy
