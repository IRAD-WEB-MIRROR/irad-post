Order of the Director General · 30 September 2026 · No. 31

On the payment of money orders

In force

In accordance with notice 2026/5 of the Bank of Irad,

1. From 30 September 2026 domestic money orders are paid out only in cash at the counter of the receiving post office or by transfer to a bank account in Irad named by the recipient.

2. Payment to wallets in mobile applications is not accepted.

3. Orders booked before 30 September with payment to such a wallet are paid in cash at the receiving office on presentation of an ID card.

4. The Deputy Director General for Payments and Subscriptions is responsible for the implementation of this order.

Director General of Irad Pochtasy
