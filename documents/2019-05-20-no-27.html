Order of the Director General · 20 May 2019 · No. 27

Rules for the provision of postal services

In force

1. Acceptance. Letters may be posted in a letterbox or handed in at a counter. Registered items, parcels and money orders are accepted only at a counter; the sender receives a receipt with the item's number.

2. Limits. A parcel may weigh up to 20 kg and measure up to 1 m on its longest side. Cash, weapons, flammable and perishable goods are not accepted.

3. Delivery. Ordinary letters are delivered to the letterbox. For registered items and parcels a notice of arrival (form P-22) is left, and the item is handed over at the post office on presentation of an ID card.

4. Storage. Items awaiting collection are kept for 30 days and then returned to the sender.

5. Collection by another person. Another person may collect an item with a power of attorney (form P-27), valid for up to one year.

6. Searches. A search for a registered item or parcel is opened on form P-15 with the receipt. The answer is given within 30 days, or within 90 days for an item from abroad.

7. Complaints. Every post office keeps a complaints book. Complaints may also be sent to the Directorate in writing.

Director General of Irad Pochtasy
