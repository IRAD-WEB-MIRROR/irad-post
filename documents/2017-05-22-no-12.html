Order of the Director General · 22 May 2017 · No. 12

On the exchange of international mail at Chu Gate

In force

1. Outgoing international mail is handed over to the Kazakh postal service at the Chu Gate exchange on Tuesdays and Fridays, from 1 June 2017.

2. Items accepted at the Talas Central Post Office by 13:00 on the day before a dispatch day go with that dispatch. District offices send international items to the Talas sorting centre the day they are accepted.

3. Every item other than a letter is accepted only with the customs declaration P-2, completed in English or Russian in two copies, and unsealed for inspection at the counter.

4. Postage on international mail is paid at the counter and printed on a label. Postage stamps of Irad are not accepted on international mail.

5. Parcels of goods are not accepted for dispatch abroad.

6. Incoming items are passed to customs at the Talas Customs Clearance Terminal before delivery.

Director General of Irad Pochtasy
