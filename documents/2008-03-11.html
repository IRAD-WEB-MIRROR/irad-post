Charter · 11 March 2008

Charter of the State Postal Enterprise Irad Pochtasy (extract)

In force

1. Name. The State Postal Enterprise Irad Pochtasy (Irad Post; in Russian ГП «Ирадпочта») is the successor of the Territorial Postal Service and holds its post offices, buildings and records.

2. Seat. The Directorate is in the Talas Central Post Office, Pochtamt Street 2, Talas.

3. Purpose. The enterprise provides the public postal service throughout the Irad Special Economic Zone at uniform prices: letters, parcels, registered and express items, money orders, post-office boxes, the payment of state pensions and allowances, newspaper subscriptions, and the exchange of international mail through a foreign postal service.

4. Stamps. The enterprise alone issues the postage stamps of Irad, valid for domestic mail, and keeps the Philatelic Bureau.

5. Emblem. The emblem of the enterprise is a white horse at full gallop on a teal disc above two yellow speed lines. The horse alone is used on postmarks.

6. Management. The enterprise is headed by the Director General and reports to the Department of Infrastructure and Communications, which approves its prices and annual report.
