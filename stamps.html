Courier definitive of the Democratic Republic (not issued)Not issued

2007-02-12 · 5 som

Printed in Kobozev for the Democratic Republic of Irad and due on sale on 26 February 2007. After the Irad Special Economic Zone was proclaimed on 25 February the sheets were withdrawn and pulped. A few came onto the market in the 2010s; the Philatelic Bureau does not sell or value them.

Church of St. Janyl, World Heritage

2004-08-24 · 37 ¢ US cents

Issued by the Territorial Postal Service on the saint's feast, after the church and the ruins of Old Iyık Janyl were inscribed on the World Heritage List. Values in US cents, for letters to the United States sent through Talas Air Base; valid until 2007.

Catalogue 2004–2026 (PDF)

Counter 1, Talas Central Post Office. Current issues at face value, first-day covers, presentation folders and the annual year set. Orders from abroad cannot be accepted.

Why do the values change?

4 som pays for an ordinary letter within Irad, 8 som for a heavier letter, 12 and 20 som for registered mail. The 1-som courier makes up the difference after a price change.
