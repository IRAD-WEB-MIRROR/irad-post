Documents and legal acts

Acts under which Irad Pochtasy works and the orders of the Director General that concern customers. Postal forms are on the Forms page.

Order of the Director General · 30 September 2026 · No. 31

On the payment of money orders

In force

Money orders are paid in cash at the counter or to a bank account only, following Bank of Irad notice 2026/5.

Order of the Director General · 12 January 2026 · No. 2

Stamp issue programme for 2026

In force

The Year of the Horse (11 March); as amended on 14 October, Toktogul: 1,440 megawatts (15 December).

Order of the Director General · 20 January 2025 · No. 3

On domestic postal prices from 1 February 2025

In force

Prices for letters, registered items, express letters and parcels within Irad, approved by the Department of Infrastructure and Communications. The current prices are on the Prices page.

Order of the Director General · 15 January 2021 · No. 4

Procedure for paying pensions and allowances at post offices

In force

Payment days, documents, payment to another person and home delivery.

Order of the Director General · 20 March 2020 · No. 9

On the work of post offices during the quarantine regime

No longer in force

Limits on customers in post office halls and the payment of pensions while executive directive 2020/05 was in force.

Order of the Director General · 20 May 2019 · No. 27

Rules for the provision of postal services

In force

Acceptance, delivery, storage and searches for letters and parcels within Irad (as amended 30 September 2026).

In force

On the organisation of the administration
