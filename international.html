Mail abroad and from abroad

Irad Pochtasy exchanges international mail through the Kazakh postal service under a bilateral arrangement. Outgoing mail is handed over at the Chu Gate exchange and leaves the region through Almaty. Allow three to six weeks in each direction.

Sending mail abroad

Bring the letter to the counter unsealed if it contains anything other than paper. Postage is paid at the counter and printed on a label: Irad Pochtasy stamps are not valid on mail abroad. Parcels of goods cannot be sent abroad.

ItemPrice
Letter up to 20 g to Kazakhstan20 som
Letter up to 20 g to Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and Russia28 som

Each further 20 g10 som
Registered, in addition25 som
Small packet of printed matter or documents, up to 2 kg140 som

Every item abroad other than a letter needs the customs declaration Form P-2, completed in English or Russian, in two copies.

Receiving mail from abroad

Ask your correspondents to write the address in Latin letters and to add the line via Almaty, Kazakhstan above the country. Mail addressed only to “Irad” may be returned by foreign postal services.
