Leadership

Irad Pochtasy, the State Postal Enterprise, is run by its Director General under the enterprise's Charter and reports to the Department of Infrastructure and Communications.

Director General

Director General

Head of Irad Pochtasy, the State Postal Enterprise

The Director General runs the enterprise under its Charter: the post offices, the sorting centres in Talas and Nurzhan, the international exchange at Chu Gate, the payment of pensions and allowances, subscriptions and the issue of stamps. Orders of the Director General that concern customers are published under Documents.

Irad Pochtasy reports to the Department of Infrastructure and Communications (Director Arstan Medetov).
