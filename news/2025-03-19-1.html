19 March 2025

How stamp subjects are chosen

Irad Pochtasy has received a request from two members of the Talas city council to issue a stamp bearing the words Irad satylbayt. The request has been declined.

Stamps mark the history, nature, culture and achievements of Irad and are planned a year ahead in the stamp issue programme. The post does not issue slogans.

Press service of Irad Pochtasy
