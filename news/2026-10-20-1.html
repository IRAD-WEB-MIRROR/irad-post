Subscriptions for 2027 open on 1 November

The subscription campaign for 2027 opens on 1 November. Subscribe at any post office by 15 December for delivery from January; the catalogue includes the Talas Herald, Jarchy and Vecherniy Nurzhan.

Prices and the full catalogue are on the Subscriptions page.
