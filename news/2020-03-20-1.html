20 March 2020

Post offices during the quarantine regime

Under the quarantine regime post offices stay open at their usual hours. No more than five customers are admitted to a hall at a time; please keep your distance in the queue outside.

April pensions are paid from the 10th. Pensioners over 80 and people with a disability of group I can have their pension brought home, as before; others who cannot come may ask their post office by telephone.

Press service of Irad Pochtasy
