17 February 2025

The year 2024 at Irad Pochtasy

In 2024 Irad Pochtasy paid pensions and allowances every month at its counters and by home delivery to some 310,000 pensioners, took the 2025 newspaper subscriptions at about 190 post offices and issued one stamp, for the Feast of St. Janyl.

The Kyzyl-Adyr office, reopened in 2023, completed its first full year after refurbishment. The enterprise's annual report goes to the Department of Infrastructure and Communications.

Press service of Irad Pochtasy
