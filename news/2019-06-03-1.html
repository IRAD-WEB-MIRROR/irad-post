3 June 2019

Next-day express letters between Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl

From 3 June express letters posted at the main offices of Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl by 12:00 are delivered to an address in any of the three cities on the next working day.

The fee is shown in the price list.

Press service of Irad Pochtasy
