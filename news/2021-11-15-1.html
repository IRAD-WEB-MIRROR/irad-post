15 November 2021

Money orders booked electronically at the main offices

Domestic money orders are now booked electronically at the main offices of Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl. The money can be collected at the receiving office the same day; the customer receives a printed receipt with the order number.

Counter staff of the three offices completed a week's training before the change. District offices continue to use form P-11.

Press service of Irad Pochtasy
