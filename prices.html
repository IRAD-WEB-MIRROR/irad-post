Prices for mail within Irad

Prices in force from 1 March 2024, including value added tax. They apply to all addresses in the ISEZ. Mail to the ATR is held for collection at the nearest post office.

Letters and postcards

ItemPrice
Postcard3 som
Letter up to 20 g4 som
Letter 20–100 g8 som
Letter or small packet 100–250 g15 som
Registered, in addition12 som
Advice of delivery, in addition6 som

ServicePrice

Forms: parcel dispatch note P-4, money order P-11, post-office box P-30; all postal forms on the Forms page.

Irad Pochtasy stamps are valid for domestic mail only. Postage for mail abroad is paid at the counter: see Mail abroad.
