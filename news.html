Press service news

Announcements and press releases of Irad Pochtasy. Short service notices are also on the home page.

2026

  • 29 October 2026
    November pensions from the 10th

    November pensions and allowances are paid from Tuesday, 10 November. On payment days the Talas Central Post Office and the main offices in Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl open at 8:00.

  • 20 October 2026
    Subscriptions for 2027 open on 1 November

    The subscription campaign for 2027 opens on 1 November. Subscribe at any post office by 15 December for delivery from January; the catalogue includes the Talas Herald, Jarchy and Vecherniy Nurzhan.

  • 16 October 2026
    Toktogul stamp on sale from 15 December

    A 12-som stamp marking the completion of the Toktogul refit, which raised the station to 1,440 megawatts, goes on sale on 15 December. First-day covers will be postmarked at the Toktogul post office.

  • 1 October 2026
    Tracking on e-Irad and a new website

    Registered letters and parcels can now be tracked on this website and on e-Irad, the public services portal; the same number gives the same result on both. International items are updated with a delay of up to three days.

  • 30 September 2026
    Money orders: cash at the counter or to a bank account

    Following Bank of Irad notice 2026/5, from today money orders are paid out only in cash at the counter or to a bank account. Payments to wallets in mobile apps are not accepted.

  • 11 March 2026
    The Year of the Horse

    Two stamps for the Year of the Horse, which opens the new twelve-year cycle, go on sale today, Irad Day: 8 som, a bay horse under Polaris, and 20 som, a grey mare and foal.

2025

  • 19 March 2025
    How stamp subjects are chosen

    Irad Pochtasy has received a request from two members of the Talas city council to issue a stamp bearing the words Irad satylbayt. The request has been declined.

  • 17 February 2025
    The year 2024 at Irad Pochtasy

    In 2024 Irad Pochtasy paid pensions and allowances every month at its counters and by home delivery to some 310,000 pensioners, took the 2025 newspaper subscriptions at about 190 post offices and issued one stamp, for the Feast…

2024

  • 24 August 2024
    Feast of St. Janyl stamp and pilgrimage postmark

    A stamp for the Feast of St. Janyl was issued today at the Iyık Janyl post office. First-day covers are stamped with a pilgrimage postmark at a counter by the pilgrims' square.

2023

  • 12 September 2023
    Kyzyl-Adyr post office reopens

    The post office in Kyzyl-Adyr, the seat of Kara-Buura District, has reopened after a summer's refurbishment: a new roof, heating and a separate counter for pensions.

2022

  • 15 May 2022
    Fauna of the Talas Alatau: the ibex

    The ibex opens a new series of stamps on the animals of the mountains. The stamp is on sale at all post offices from today.

2021

  • 15 November 2021
    Money orders booked electronically at the main offices

    Domestic money orders are now booked electronically at the main offices of Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl. The money can be collected at the receiving office the same day; the customer receives a printed receipt with the order…

  • 21 June 2021
    Music of Irad: the komuz

    A stamp showing the komuz is issued today, the day of the Talas komuz festival. A special postmark is used at the festival post counter.

2020

  • 20 March 2020
    Post offices during the quarantine regime

    Under the quarantine regime post offices stay open at their usual hours. No more than five customers are admitted to a hall at a time; please keep your distance in the queue outside.

2019

  • 14 September 2019
    Horses of Irad: the Akyel

    The first stamp of the new Horses of Irad series, showing the golden Akyel, the riding breed of the Talas valley, is on sale from today.

  • 3 June 2019
    Next-day express letters between Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl

    From 3 June express letters posted at the main offices of Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl by 12:00 are delivered to an address in any of the three cities on the next working day.

2018

  • 8 October 2018
    New parcel line at the Talas sorting centre

    A new parcel sorting line has been brought into service at the Talas sorting centre. Parcels handed in at the Talas Central Post Office by 15:00 now leave for the district offices the same evening.

  • 11 March 2018
    Irad Day stamp: the Governor and Zharkyn

    A commemorative stamp showing the Governor with his stallion Zharkyn, from a photograph taken after a race in 2014, goes on sale today, Irad Day, at all post offices.

2017

  • 1 November 2017
    Subscriptions for 2018 open

    The subscription campaign for 2018 runs from 1 November to 15 December at every post office. Subscribers who sign up by 15 December receive their newspapers from the first January issue.

2016

  • 26 December 2016
    Post offices over the New Year holiday

    On 1 January all post offices are closed. On 2 January the Talas Central Post Office opens 10:00–14:00 for letters and stamps only; all other offices reopen on 3 January at their usual hours.

  • 4 April 2016
    Post-office boxes at the Iyık Janyl Main Office

    From 4 April post-office boxes can be rented at Post Office No. 3, Iyık Janyl, as they already can at the Talas Central Post Office and the Nurzhan Main Office. Eighty boxes have been fitted in the hall by the pilgrims' square.
