Questions and answers

Answers to the questions our helpline hears most often. Prices are on the Prices page.

Letters and parcels

My parcel is late. What should I do?

Check the tracking number on the Track page. If there is no news for two weeks, fill in a search request (form P-15) at any post office with your receipt. We reply within 30 days, or 90 days for items from abroad.

Can I track an ordinary letter?

No. Only registered letters and parcels have a tracking number. Send the letter registered if you need proof of delivery.

How long is an item kept at the post office?

30 days from the notice of arrival (form P-22). After that it is returned to the sender.

Can someone else collect my parcel?

Yes, with a power of attorney on form P-27, certified at the post office and valid for up to one year, and his or her own ID card.

How do I rent a post-office box?

Boxes are available at the main offices in Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl. Apply on form P-30 at the counter.

I received an SMS asking me to pay to release a parcel. Is it from you?

No. Irad Pochtasy never asks for card numbers or payment by SMS or messenger. Such messages are fraud; do not follow the link.

Mail abroad

Why does mail from abroad take so long?

International mail reaches Irad through the Kazakh postal service, by way of Almaty and the Chu Gate exchange, and parcels pass customs in Talas. Allow three to six weeks.

Can I use Irad stamps on a letter abroad?

No. Irad stamps are valid for domestic mail only. Postage abroad is paid at the counter and printed on a label.

Can I send a parcel of goods abroad?

No. Only letters and small packets of printed matter or documents up to 2 kg are accepted for abroad, with a customs declaration (form P-2).

Pensions and payments

When are pensions paid?

From the 10th of each month, or the next working day if the 10th is a Sunday or holiday. Queues are shortest from the third day.

Can my pension be brought home?

Yes, free of charge, if you are over 80 or have a disability of group I. Apply once at your post office with a medical certificate.

Can a money order be paid to my phone wallet?

No. Since 30 September 2026 money orders are paid in cash at the counter or to a bank account only.

Stamps and subscriptions

Can I order stamps from abroad?

No. The Philatelic Bureau, counter 1 of the Talas Central Post Office, sells current issues at face value at the counter only and cannot accept orders from abroad.

When should I subscribe to newspapers for next year?

From 1 November to 15 December for delivery from January, or by the 20th of any month for the following month.
