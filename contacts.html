Contacts and reception

The Directorate of Irad Pochtasy is in the Talas Central Post Office. For a lost or late item, or a complaint about a post office, see Contact and complaints.

Written appeals

Written appeals to the Director General can be sent by post to the Directorate (Pochtamt Street 2, Talas), handed in at the reception on the 2nd floor or e-mailed to help@post.iradgov.org. Give your name, address and, for a postal item, its tracking number and the receipt. We reply within 30 days.

Searches for lost items and complaints about a post office: Contact and complaints.

How to get here

The Central Post Office stands west of the central square. The Directorate's entrance is from the courtyard, through the gate on the left of the main building.
