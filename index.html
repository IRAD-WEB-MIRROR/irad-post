Notices

  • 2026-10-20The 2027 subscription campaign opens on 1 November. Subscribe at any post office by 15 December for delivery from January.
  • 2026-10-16A 12-som stamp for the completion of the Toktogul refit goes on sale on 15 December. First-day covers at the Toktogul post office.
  • 2026-10-05October pensions are paid from the 10th. On payment days the Talas Central Post Office and the main offices in Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl open at 8:00.
  • 2026-10-01Items can now also be tracked on e-Irad, the public services portal. International items are updated with a delay of up to three days.
  • 2026-09-30Following Bank of Irad notice 2026/5, money orders are paid out in cash at the counter or to a bank account only. Payments to app wallets are not accepted.
